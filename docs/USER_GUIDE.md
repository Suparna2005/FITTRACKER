1. **Open the App Link** in your browser:
   - **Public Secure HTTPS URL:** Check active Cloudflare HTTPS link (or `http://187.127.178.161:3010`, which auto-redirects to HTTPS).

## 2. Accessing the App & Installing as a Mobile App (PWA)

IronForge AI is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** that runs smoothly on any smartphone, tablet, or laptop without needing an app store download.

### How to Install on Your Phone:
- **On Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge):**
  1. Open the app URL in Chrome.
  2. Tap the **three dots menu (`⋮`)** in the top right corner.
  3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. The IronForge AI icon will appear on your phone's home screen and open full-screen like a native app.
- **On iPhone (Safari):**
  1. Open the app URL in **Safari**.
  2. Tap the **Share icon** (square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
- **On Laptop / PC (Chrome / Edge):**
  1. Click the **Install icon** in the browser URL bar (or click `⋮` > "Save and share" > "Install IronForge AI").

> **Camera Security Context Notice (HTTPS):**  
> Modern mobile browsers (iOS Safari, Android Chrome) strictly require an encrypted **HTTPS** connection to activate hardware webcams via `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia`. The outward server is equipped with automated HTTPS redirection so camera permissions work cleanly without security warnings.

## 7. Module 1: AI Vision Biomechanics Camera Coach

The Vision Coach uses on-device computer vision (MediaPipe) to track your joints at 60 FPS, detect rep depth, and correct your posture in real time.

```
       [ CAMERA / WEBCAM ]
                │
         [ AI SKELETON ]
    ┌───────────┼───────────┐
    ▼           ▼           ▼
🟢 GREEN     🔴 RED      🟡 YELLOW
Optimal    Correction   Joint Angles
  Form       Warning      (HUD 142°)
```

### Camera Setup & Phone Placement
1. **Distance:** Place your phone or laptop on a flat surface or tripod **1.5 to 2.5 meters (5 to 8 feet)** away from you.
2. **Angle:** Set the camera at approximately waist or chest height.
3. **Visibility:** Ensure your full body (head to feet) is visible in the camera frame.
4. **Lighting:** Face towards a well-lit area; avoid standing directly in front of bright backlights or windows.
5. **Camera Switcher:** On smartphones, tap **"🔄 Flip Camera"** in the top right to switch between the front selfie camera and the rear camera.

### Hands-Free Gesture Controls
You never have to touch your sweaty screen during a set! The Vision Coach responds to natural hand gestures:

| Gesture | Visual Indicator | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raise Hand / Open Palm (`🖐️`)** | Glowing cyan skeleton, palm detected | Triggers set countdown from distance |
| **Two Fingers Up (`✌️`)** | `✌️ 2 FINGERS` badge | Starts workout set immediately |
| **Closed Fist (`✊`)** | `✊ FIST` badge | Pauses or stops the active set |
| **1, 2, or 3 Fingers** | `1 / 2 / 3` indicator | Selects Set 1, Set 2, or Set 3 hands-free |

### Understanding the Color Signals

| Signal | Color | What It Means | What to Do |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Right Posture** | `🟢 Green` | **Optimal Form:** Spine is neutral, knees track correctly, and depth achieved. | Continue your repetitions with smooth cadence. |
| **Correction Warning** | `🔴 Red` | **Form Breakdown:** Knees buckling inward, lower back rounding, or short reps. | Adjust your body immediately according to the cue. |
| **Joint Angles HUD** | `🟡 Yellow` | **Live Biomechanical Degree Indicators:** Glowing angle arcs over knees, hips, elbows. | Observe range of motion on the HUD strip. |
| **Calibrating Setup** | `⚪ White` | **Ready Hold:** Verifying steady starting posture before movement starts. | Hold starting position for half a second. |

### Voice AI Commands
If your microphone is enabled, you can command the AI with your voice:
- Say **"Start set"** or **"Go"** $\to$ Begins rep tracking immediately.
- Say **"Pause set"** or **"Stop"** $\to$ Halts the active counter.
- Say **"Set 1"**, **"Set 2"**, **"Set 3"** $\to$ Switches target set hands-free.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting (FAQ)

### Q1: The camera screen is black or asking for permission.
- **Fix:** Browsers require HTTPS to access webcams. Ensure you are on the secure HTTPS link. If prompted, tap **Allow**. If accidentally denied, tap the padlock icon in your browser URL bar, set Camera to **Allow**, and refresh.

### Q2: Why are my repetitions not counting?
- **Check 1 (Visibility):** Ensure your full body (head to toes) is visible in frame from 1.5 to 2.5 meters away.
- **Check 2 (Setup Lockout):** Stand still in the starting position for 1 second until the badge turns `🟢 READY`.
- **Check 3 (Full Depth):** Achieve complete range of motion (e.g., parallel squat depth). Incomplete half-reps are flagged as `⚠️ NO REP: INCOMPLETE DEPTH` to protect form integrity.

### Q3: How do I flip between selfie and rear cameras on mobile?
- **Fix:** Tap the **"🔄 Flip Camera"** button in the top right corner of the camera HUD.

### Q4: How does the AI Doctor modify my workouts when injured?
- **Fix:** The recovery engine automatically substitutes aggravating compound exercises with joint-friendly movements and attaches an `ADAPTED FOR RECOVERY` shield to your workout plan.

### Q5: Can I access my data across multiple devices?
- **Yes!** Your profile, workout logs, splits, and meal data sync in real time to the central database. Log in with your email or Google account on any phone, tablet, or PC anytime.
